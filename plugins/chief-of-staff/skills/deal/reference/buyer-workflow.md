# Buyer workflow, the step reference

_Built 2026-11-26 from the Reframe Lab buyer workflow sheet, as grouped on 2026-11-18. Every row of the sheet is here as a step or a checkbox. Roles are the sheet's. Due rules are relative to a date the deal file records. This is what the deal skills infer against; nothing here is a form to fill in._

Role shorthand: **A** Agent · **TC** Transaction Coordinator · **AS** Assistant · **TL** Team Lead

Dates this workflow keys off: Appraisal deadline, Appraisal objection deadline, Appraisal result, Buyer agreement signed on, Buyer name(s), Buyer status, Closing date, Consultation date, Contingent on selling the buyer's current home?, Contract acceptance date, Did we reach a resolution?, Earnest money deadline, Final walkthrough date, Financing contingency deadline, Follow-up specialists needed, HOA document delivery deadline, HOA review and objection deadline, Home inspection deadline, How is the buyer paying?, Inspection objection deadline, Inspection resolution deadline, Is there an HOA or condo association?, Loan commitment deadline, Other offers on this home?, Pool or spa?, Possession date, Pre-approved or proof of funds in hand?, Property address (once under contract), Radon and specialty inspection deadline, Sale of buyer's home deadline, Survey deadline, Title commitment delivery deadline, Title objection deadline, Title resolution deadline, Was the home built before 1978?, Water supply


## First conversation

- **Log the new buyer and reply quickly** (A; due 1 business days after the run starts)
  - [ ] Save the source, contact details, timeline and the type of home they want to the client file
  - [ ] Reply by text within 2 hours (9:30 am to 7 pm), or by email by the next business day
  - note: Why it matters: a fast, clear first reply sets the tone for the whole search.
- **Hold the first call and send the pre-consultation package** (A; due 2 business days after the run starts)
  - [ ] Cover timeline, financing status, motivation, current living situation, and whether they work with another agent
  - [ ] Send the buyer guide, an overview of how we work as advisors, and the pre-approval checklist
- **Book the buyer consultation** (AS; due 3 business days after the run starts; records: Consultation date)
  - note: Confirm the date, time and place, then send a calendar invite with the agenda.
- **Confirm the buyer's financing before the consultation** (A; due 1 days before Consultation date; records: How is the buyer paying?, records: Pre-approved or proof of funds in hand?)
  - note: Pre-approval letter, or proof of funds for a cash purchase, and a confirmed budget range.

## Buyer consultation

- **Explain how we work and what we owe the buyer** (A; due the day of Consultation date)
  - [ ] Share our experience and what makes our approach different
  - [ ] Explain fiduciary duty: full disclosure, honest guidance on fair market value, how we negotiate
  - note: Why it matters: the buyer should know who works for them, and how.
- **Learn what the buyer needs and can afford** (A; due the day of Consultation date)
  - [ ] Needs assessment: motivation, timeline, must-haves, absolutely-nots, neighborhoods, readiness from 1 to 10
  - [ ] Budget: a comfortable monthly payment, down payment sources, loan types, closing cost estimates
- **Walk through the process, the market and our buyer services** (A; due the day of Consultation date)
  - [ ] Every step from search to closing, with realistic expectations
  - [ ] Current inventory, competition, pricing trends and typical timelines
  - [ ] Our services: concierge search, hand-picked collections, off-market access, buyer cards for open houses
- **Cover agency, dual agency and fair housing** (A; due the day of Consultation date)
  - [ ] Provide the state agency disclosure and explain the types of agency
  - [ ] Explain our dual agency policy and get written consent where it is allowed
  - [ ] Review fair housing: protected classes, no steering, equal service
  - note: Connecticut and Colorado use different forms. Use the current state form.
- **Get the signed agency disclosure acknowledgment** (AS; due the day of Consultation date)
  - note: Most states require it before any detailed discussion. File it in the client record.
- **Set communication preferences** (A; due the day of Consultation date)
  - note: Agree on the best way to reach the buyer, response times, the weekly update day and how tours get booked. Who handles what: the agent takes notes so the assistant can save every preference to the client file.

## After the consultation

- **Send the consultation recap and next steps** (AS; due the day of Consultation date)
  - note: Summary of needs, timeline, action items and links to resources.
- **Set up property alerts** (A; due 1 days after Consultation date)
  - note: Match the criteria from the needs assessment and the alert frequency the buyer prefers.
- **Introduce a trusted lender** (A; due 1 days after Consultation date)
  - note: A warm introduction that shares the buyer's timeline and goals.
- **Save the buyer's profile and status** (AS; due 2 days after Consultation date; records: Buyer status)
  - note: Save the profile to the client file and set the right follow-up plan.
- **Set 30, 60 and 90 day check-ins** (AS; due 3 days after Consultation date)
  - note: If the buyer isn't ready yet, we stay helpful: useful market updates and a check-in at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Buyer agreement

- **Review the buyer agreement and how compensation works** (A; due 2 days after Consultation date)
  - [ ] Explain buyer representation: what we do, loyalty, fiduciary duty
  - [ ] Present the written buyer agreement; it is required before any tour
  - [ ] Explain how we are paid, who pays, and that the terms are negotiable
  - [ ] Set a specific dollar amount or percentage; open-ended terms are not allowed
  - [ ] Agree on what happens if a seller offers no buyer-agent compensation
- **Get the signed buyer agreement** (AS; due 3 days after Consultation date; records: Buyer agreement signed on)
  - note: Every buyer signs before any showing. Nothing after this step starts until it is done.
- **File the signed agreement** (TC; due the day of Buyer agreement signed on)
  - note: Upload it to the buyer's file in reZEN and note the term and dates.
- **Confirm the buyer paperwork is on file** (A; due 2 days after Buyer agreement signed on)
  - [ ] Signed buyer agreement
  - [ ] Agency disclosure acknowledgment
  - [ ] Fair housing acknowledgment
  - [ ] Pre-approval letter
  - [ ] Proof of funds, for a cash purchase

## Finding the home

- **Set up a focused search** (A; due 2 days after Buyer agreement signed on)
  - [ ] Refine the criteria: must-haves, nice-to-haves, deal-breakers
  - [ ] Build hand-picked collections by neighborhood, price or style
  - [ ] Watch for off-market and coming-soon homes through our network
  - [ ] Show the buyer how to read listing photos and descriptions for red flags
  - [ ] Keep every location and school preference coming from the buyer, in writing
- **Give the buyer our cards for open houses** (AS; due 3 days after Buyer agreement signed on)
  - note: So they can visit open houses on their own and the listing agent knows they are represented.
- **Tour homes and capture the buyer's feedback** (A)
  - [ ] Book showings: top picks right away, others within 24 hours or on a planned tour day
  - [ ] Prepare a packet for each home: MLS sheet, tax records, HOA, days on market, price history
  - [ ] Give a straight read on structure, systems and cosmetics
  - [ ] Note visible concerns for the inspection and talk through likely costs
  - [ ] Record ratings, pros and cons after every tour
  - note: Repeats for as long as the search runs.
- **Narrow to the top three and research them** (A)
  - [ ] Rank homes against the buyer's priorities and drop the ones that miss core needs
  - [ ] Run a pricing analysis on each of the top three, walked through live
  - [ ] Research the neighborhood: schools, commute, amenities, future development, HOA
  - [ ] Review each home's history: past sales, days on market, price changes
  - [ ] Estimate the true monthly cost: taxes, insurance, HOA, utilities, maintenance

## Making an offer

- **Build the offer strategy** (A)
  - [ ] Call the lender: the pre-approval is current and covers this price (or proof of funds for cash)
  - [ ] Agree on price, contingencies, closing date, deposit, escalation and concessions
  - [ ] Recommend a price from the pricing analysis, the asking price and the competition
  - [ ] Decide how buyer-agent compensation is handled in this offer
- **Prepare the offer and review it with the buyer** (A)
  - [ ] Prepare the contract with every form, addendum and disclosure, every field checked
  - [ ] Prepare the estimated closing cost worksheet
  - [ ] Walk through every term so the buyer understands the obligations and timelines
- **Collect signatures, submit the offer and confirm receipt** (AS; records: Other offers on this home?)
  - [ ] Every buyer signs the offer and addenda; nothing left blank
  - [ ] Submit with the pre-approval letter or proof of funds, and a cover letter if it helps
  - [ ] Confirm receipt and ask about other offers and the seller's timeline
- **Plan for a multiple-offer situation** (A)
  - note: Highest and best, escalation clauses, appraisal gap coverage: the buyer hears the options and the risks.
- **Negotiate the seller's response** (A)
  - [ ] Share the seller's response right away and agree on the next move
  - [ ] Negotiate calmly toward terms that work for both sides, and keep the buyer informed
- **Confirm mutual acceptance and the property details** (A; records: Is there an HOA or condo association?, records: Was the home built before 1978?, records: Water supply, records: Pool or spa?, records: Contingent on selling the buyer's current home?)
  - note: Every signature, initial and date in place, no blanks, all addenda attached. These answers show or hide the steps that fit this home.
- **Record the contract dates** (TC; records: Contract acceptance date, records: Earnest money deadline, records: Home inspection deadline, records: Inspection objection deadline, records: Inspection resolution deadline, records: Radon and specialty inspection deadline, records: Appraisal deadline, records: Appraisal objection deadline, records: Title commitment delivery deadline, records: Title objection deadline, records: Title resolution deadline, records: HOA document delivery deadline, records: HOA review and objection deadline, records: Survey deadline, records: Loan commitment deadline, records: Financing contingency deadline, records: Sale of buyer's home deadline, records: Final walkthrough date, records: Closing date, records: Possession date)
  - note: Enter every deadline from the executed contract and share the calendar with the buyer. These dates set the due dates for everything that follows, so leave a field blank only if the contract doesn't have it.
- **Share the contract and explain the deadlines** (AS; due 1 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - [ ] Send the executed contract to the TC, lender and title company or closing attorney; file it in reZEN
  - [ ] Walk the buyer through every deadline; missing one can cost rights or the deposit
- **Confirm the earnest money arrived** (TC; due the day of Earnest money deadline)
  - note: Who handles what: the escrow holder contacts the buyer for the deposit; the TC confirms it arrived on time.

## Inspections

- **Book the home inspection and confirm access** (TC; due 2 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - [ ] Book right away; inspection periods are often 7 to 10 days
  - [ ] Confirm access with the listing agent: attic, crawlspace and garage open
- **Book the standard specialty inspections** (TC; due 3 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - [ ] Radon test, results in 48 to 72 hours
  - [ ] Sewer scope for public sewer, or a full septic inspection
  - [ ] Pest and termite inspection
- **Book a well water test** (TC; due 3 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Water quality, flow rate and potability. Many lenders require it.
- **Book a pool or spa inspection** (TC; due 3 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Equipment, safety compliance and structure.
- **Attend the inspection with the buyer** (A; due the day of Home inspection deadline)
  - note: Walk through with the inspector, help the buyer understand what they're seeing, and take notes and photos.
- **Review the report and price the big items** (A; due 2 days before Inspection objection deadline; records: Follow-up specialists needed)
  - note: Sort findings into safety, structural, mechanical and cosmetic, discuss how serious each is, and get contractor estimates for anything significant.
- **Book a structural engineer** (TC; due 1 days before Inspection objection deadline)
- **Book a roof inspection** (TC; due 1 days before Inspection objection deadline)
  - note: Document age, condition, remaining life and replacement cost.
- **Prepare and submit the repair request** (A; due the day of Inspection objection deadline)
  - [ ] Put safety and major items first, with documentation; keep it reasonable
  - [ ] Submit to the listing agent within the contract timeframe
- **Negotiate the inspection resolution** (A; due the day of Inspection resolution deadline; records: Did we reach a resolution?)
  - note: Repairs, credits, a price change, or a mix. Everything in writing.
- **Sign the inspection resolution amendment** (A; due the day of Inspection resolution deadline)
  - note: Everyone signs, and it spells out what the seller will repair and to what standard.
- **Advise the buyer on termination rights** (A; due the day of Inspection resolution deadline)
  - note: Review the contract's termination terms, protect the earnest money, and act inside the deadline.

## Title, appraisal and financing

- **Confirm the title order is open** (TC; due 3 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Make sure the title company or closing attorney has the contract and has started.
- **Review the title commitment with the buyer** (A; due the day of Title objection deadline)
  - [ ] Check for liens, easements, boundary issues and exceptions
  - [ ] Explain any issue and whether it can be fixed before closing
  - [ ] Send a written title objection within the deadline, if needed
- **Confirm the appraisal is ordered** (TC; due 7 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Follow up with the lender and track the order date and expected completion.
- **Send comparable sales to the appraiser, where allowed** (A; due 10 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Rules on contacting the appraiser vary by market. Check before sending.
- **Review the appraisal** (A; due the day of Appraisal deadline; records: Appraisal result)
- **Work through a low appraisal** (A; due the day of Appraisal objection deadline)
  - [ ] Lay out the options: renegotiate, cover the gap, split it, challenge it, or walk away
  - [ ] Negotiate with the listing agent using additional comps
- **Confirm loan documents and track underwriting** (TC; due 7 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - [ ] Confirm the buyer sent everything the lender asked for: tax returns, pay stubs, statements
  - [ ] Check in with the lender every week: submission, processing, underwriting, approval
- **Keep the buyer quick with lender requests** (A; due 7 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Every slow reply to the lender can push the closing date.
- **Confirm the loan commitment** (TC; due the day of Loan commitment deadline)
  - note: Track every condition and make sure each one is cleared before closing.
- **Line up homeowner's insurance** (TC; due 14 days before Closing date)
  - note: The buyer chooses the carrier and coverage, and the binder goes to the lender before closing. Who handles what: the TC shares a list of local insurance agents from the agent or broker; the buyer contacts them.
- **Explain the title insurance options** (A; due 14 days before Closing date)
  - note: Owner's policy, lender's policy and extra coverage options, and why each matters.
- **Receive the HOA documents** (TC; due the day of HOA document delivery deadline)
- **Review the HOA documents with the buyer** (A; due the day of HOA review and objection deadline)
  - [ ] Fees, restrictions, financial health, pending litigation, rental and pet rules
  - [ ] Send a written objection within the deadline if anything is unacceptable

## Contingencies

- **Send the weekly deadline reminder** (TC; due 7 days after Contract acceptance date)
  - note: Upcoming deadlines, what is needed and who is responsible. Every week until closing.
- **Watch every contingency deadline** (TC; due 10 days before Closing date)
  - [ ] Inspection: objection filed or contingency removed in time
  - [ ] Appraisal: ordered, completed and resolved in time
  - [ ] Financing: loan approval on track; escalate if the lender threatens the date
  - [ ] Title: commitment received, reviewed and objections filed in time
  - [ ] HOA: documents received and reviewed inside the review period
- **Track the sale of the buyer's current home** (TC; due the day of Sale of buyer's home deadline)
  - note: Listing status, offers and closing timeline, plus any kick-out clause.
- **Explain what removing a contingency means** (A)
  - note: Once a contingency is removed, the buyer may lose the right to cancel and recover the deposit.
- **Get each contingency resolved in writing** (TC; due 5 days before Closing date)
  - [ ] File a written removal or waiver for each contingency
  - [ ] Audit the file for anything still open before closing
- **Handle amendments and keep the log** (TC)
  - [ ] Process price, date or contingency changes in writing with signatures
  - [ ] Keep a clear log of original and amended terms
  - note: As needed through closing.

## Getting to closing

- **Confirm the closing date, time and place with everyone** (TC; due 7 days before Closing date)
  - note: Title company or closing attorney, buyer, seller's agent and lender. Send calendar invites.
- **Connect the buyer with our utility concierge** (TC; due 7 days before Closing date)
  - note: Electric, gas, water, internet and trash in the buyer's name from closing day. Who handles what: the TC connects the buyer with our utility concierge, who sets it up.
- **Send the moving checklist** (AS; due 10 days before Closing date)
  - note: Change of address, school enrollment, mail forwarding, DMV and voter registration.
- **Verify clear to close** (TC; due 4 days before Closing date)
  - note: Final loan approval with no open conditions. Follow up with the lender directly and escalate any delay.
- **Check the closing disclosure and closing package** (TC; due 3 days before Closing date)
  - [ ] Check price, loan amount, credits, prorations, closing costs and compensation
  - [ ] Get receipts, photos and warranties for the seller's repairs from the listing agent
  - [ ] Send title or the closing attorney everything needed: ID, lender documents, HOA forms, insurance binder
  - [ ] Schedule the final walkthrough with the listing agent; the seller should be moved out
  - note: Who handles what: the TC gets the closing disclosure and gives it a once-over; the agent and buyer review it in depth.
- **Send the closing day checklist** (AS; due 3 days before Closing date)
  - note: Photo ID, certified funds or wire confirmation, proof of insurance, and anything else requested.
- **Walk the buyer through the closing disclosure** (A; due 2 days before Closing date)
  - note: Every charge and credit, compared against the original estimate and the lender's credits.
- **Confirm the closing funds are ready** (TC; due 2 days before Closing date)
  - note: Exact amount from the closing disclosure. Wire fraud targets closings, so verify wire instructions by phone using a known number. Who handles what: the lender or closing attorney and the agent confirm wire instructions with the buyer. The TC confirms the funds are set.
- **Confirm the paperwork is on file before closing** (TC; due 2 days before Closing date)
  - [ ] Earnest money receipt
  - [ ] Seller disclosures
  - [ ] Lead-based paint disclosure, if built before 1978
  - [ ] Home inspection report
  - [ ] Radon results
  - [ ] Sewer or septic report
  - [ ] Pest report
  - [ ] Other specialty reports
  - [ ] Title commitment
  - [ ] Survey
  - [ ] Appraisal report, if financed
  - [ ] Loan application confirmation, if financed
  - [ ] Conditional loan approval, if financed
  - [ ] Clear to close, if financed
  - [ ] Homeowner's insurance binder
  - [ ] HOA package and transfer fee, if there is an HOA
  - [ ] Closing disclosure
  - [ ] Wire or cashier's check confirmation
  - [ ] Final walkthrough confirmation
- **Confirm the negotiation paperwork is on file** (A; due 2 days before Closing date)
  - [ ] Inspection repair request
  - [ ] Inspection resolution agreement
  - [ ] Title objection, if one was sent
  - [ ] Appraisal objection or resolution, if needed
  - [ ] HOA review or objection, if needed
- **Do the final walkthrough with the buyer** (A; due the day of Final walkthrough date)
  - [ ] Repairs done, condition unchanged, every included item present, no new damage
  - [ ] Systems, fixtures and appliances working; the home is clean
  - [ ] Document any issue, negotiate with the listing agent, and decide whether closing proceeds

## Closing day

- **Be at the closing with the buyer** (A; due the day of Closing date)
  - [ ] Attend in person or coordinate remote signing
  - [ ] Review the deed, mortgage, note, settlement statement and title insurance before signing
  - [ ] Check the closing disclosure against the contract, amendments and the earlier version
  - [ ] Receive and inventory the keys, remotes, codes and access items
  - [ ] Celebrate: a photo at the table or with the keys
- **Confirm signatures, funding, recording and disbursement** (TC; due the day of Closing date)
  - [ ] Every document signed; the closing package is complete
  - [ ] The lender funded and the deed is recorded
  - [ ] Compensation paid out as the agreement says, documented in the file

## After closing

- **Finish the transaction file** (TC; due 7 days after Closing date)
  - [ ] Audit the file: every document uploaded and compliant
  - [ ] Send the sale details so the client record can be updated
  - [ ] Confirm the buyer has copies: closing disclosure, deed, title policy, warranty
  - [ ] File copies of the disbursement checks or wires
  - [ ] Recorded deed, title insurance policy and final closing package on file
  - note: Who handles what: the TC doesn't work inside the agent's client database, so the agent's team updates it. The closing company usually hands the buyer copies at closing. The agent or broker confirms compensation arrived.
- **Send a closing gift and the new-home resource package** (AS; due 7 days after Closing date)
  - [ ] Closing gift and handwritten thank-you, personal to this buyer
  - [ ] Local guide, home maintenance calendar, recommended contractors, community information
- **Introduce local resources and home services** (A; due 14 days after Closing date)
  - [ ] Neighborhood favorites: restaurants, services, parks, community events
  - [ ] Design and renovation help, if the buyer wants it
  - [ ] A pre-move-in 3D scan to document the home for insurance
- **Ask how we did** (AS; due 14 days after Closing date)
  - [ ] Send the short satisfaction survey
  - [ ] Invite a review on Google or Zillow with direct links; follow up once
- **Stay in their corner after the move** (AS; due 28 days after Closing date)
  - [ ] Ask whether anyone they know could use our help
  - [ ] Set up seasonal maintenance reminders
  - [ ] Schedule a home anniversary note every year
  - [ ] Send home value updates every quarter
  - [ ] Check in once a year on maintenance needs
